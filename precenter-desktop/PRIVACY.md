# Privacy Policy

Disciples PreCenter runs on your computer. Songs, services, settings and
files are stored locally and are not sent to the developers.

The app connects to the internet only to:
- load or update the PreCenter page from the project's hosted site;
- check GitHub for new program versions;
- sign in through the Disciples site, if you choose to sign in;
- look up Bible text, if you use those features.
- sign in to an invitation-only account (stored in Firebase, a Google service), so that a team can share its song library and settings;

We do not sell data or use analytics or advertising trackers.
Those services have their own privacy policies.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/markebora/precenter/issues
