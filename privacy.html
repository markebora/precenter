Disciples PreCenter

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Disciples PreCenter is free, open-source presentation software for church services. You can use it as a guest without an account.

What stays on your device

Services, settings, media and a copy of your songs are stored on your computer or in your browser. Your sign-in is also kept there until you sign out.

Google sign-in

If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your name, email address, profile picture address and Google account identifier. They are used only to create your account, sign you in and keep your songs with your account. We do not get your Google password or access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendar. Accounts are stored by Supabase.

Your songs

While you are signed in with Google, the songs in your library, including songs saved before you signed in, are backed up to an online database stored by Supabase. This includes titles, lyrics, slide design and song details. Other users cannot see them. The people who run the service have technical access to the database and look at content only to run or protect the service, handle your request or a rights complaint, or comply with the law. Do not put sensitive personal information in songs.

Team accounts

Invited team members can also sign in with email and password. Those accounts and the data the team shares, such as its song library, settings and remote control messages, are stored in Firebase, a Google service.

Other connections

The app connects to its hosting service (Vercel) to load pages, to GitHub to check for desktop updates, and to outside services for features you use, such as Bible text, fonts and Planning Center. These services receive ordinary connection data such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

How we use data

We do not sell your data and do not use advertising or analytics trackers. Data is disclosed only with the services listed here, to run the app, or when the law requires it. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your account and backed-up songs until you ask us to delete them. To have your account or songs removed, contact us. Copies on your own devices stay until you remove them. Signing out removes the sign-in from your device but does not delete your account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and date will appear on this page.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/markebora/precenter/issues. Issues are public, so do not include private details in them.
